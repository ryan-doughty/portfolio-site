Welcome to my little site. Currently, I work at Great American Insurance Group and Yellowberri Media.

At Great American, I started out as a stylist in the Agent and Insured Experience team; writing CSS inside the Unqork platform. That job evolved into configuration work inside Unqork; connecting our backend to our front end code and acting as a lead voice in terms of styling and UX feasibiltiy. Then my role evolved into a full stack position as the AIE application became a Vue JS/Java/Springboot application. My best attribute is taking on whatever tasks come my way and jumping in no matter the complexity.

At Yellowberri, the majority of my work consists of making updates and fixing cosmetic issues with client sites. Sometimes I'm asked to resolve backend issues and some content editing, too. I truly enjoy getting the opportunity to work on a a variety of sites. All sites are Wordpress and built with Divi, though I'm not afraid to jump into a legacy site built with a custom theme.
